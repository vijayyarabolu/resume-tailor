Senior Machine Learning Engineer
Acme Logistics (made-up company for the demo)
Location: Seattle, WA (Hybrid, 2 days a week in the office)

About the role
Acme Logistics moves freight for 3,000 shippers. We are hiring a Senior Machine Learning Engineer to own model
training, deployment and monitoring for our pricing and capacity forecasts.

What you will do
- Build and run ML pipelines with Python, PyTorch and Airflow on AWS (SageMaker, Lambda).
- Serve models behind REST APIs on Kubernetes with CI/CD and Docker.
- Monitor drift and latency with MLflow and Datadog; run A/B tests on model changes.
- Work with product and operations teams to turn forecasts into decisions.

Requirements
- 5+ years of experience with Python and SQL in production ML.
- Hands-on MLOps: model deployment, experiment tracking, feature stores.
- Experience with Snowflake or Redshift.

Nice to have
- Spark or Databricks.
- LLMs and RAG for internal tools.
- Time series forecasting.
